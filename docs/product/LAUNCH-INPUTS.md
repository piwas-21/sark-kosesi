# Product and launch inputs

The business baseline is Piwas, Netherlands-held inventory, EU-wide intent and a narrow floor-seating catalogue with foam/covers-only options. Everything below is evidence to obtain, not an invented product promise. Owner-dependent items should not stop independent technical proof.

| Input | Owner / source | Required evidence | Blocks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Brand/domain/legal identity | Piwas | Final name, registration/invoice facts, domain ownership, clearance where needed | Public launch/provider website profile |
| SKU/component model | Supplier + warehouse | Real kit contents; separable/prepacked decision; receiving/quarantine units | Live stock/public product |
| Dimensions and tolerances | Supplier + measured samples | Seat/back/bolster footprint, real total dimensions, foam density/firmness, packaging dimensions/weight/count | Fit claims, freight quotes, publication |
| Fibre/care/safety/traceability | Supplier/importer/adviser | Documented composition/care, manufacturer/importer contact, batch/product identifiers and applicable compliance | Sale/public claims |
| Photographs and licences | Piwas/supplier | Actual SKU images, colour variants, usage rights, release records | Product merchandising/feed |
| Import/landed cost | Piwas/importer/accountant | Classification, freight/duties/insurance, fees, damage/returns reserve, margin model | Retail pricing |
| Mollie retail profile | Piwas/provider | Same legal-entity confirmation, approved new website profile, independent test/live keys/methods, payout readiness | Live payment |
| Tax/invoices/credit notes | Accountant | Destination VAT/OSS and refund allocation treatment, invoice/credit format and retention | Live totals/accounting |
| 27 EU market matrix | Carrier + Piwas/adviser | Every country’s zones/postcodes/islands, parcels/bulky delivery/access, costs, delivery estimates, returns, language/policy and packaging obligations | Country checkout activation |
| Email | Piwas/provider | Separate verified sender domain, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, templates/unsubscribe, bounce/complaint policy | Transactional/marketing delivery |
| Native translations | Qualified reviewers | Catalogue, checkout, legal, emails and support coverage; Arabic RTL/Kurmanji terminology | Locale publication/indexing |
| Customer operations | Piwas | Return inspection, support response promises, contact channel, staffing/roles | Launch operations |
| Hosting/recovery | Engineering + Piwas | Isolation/capacity plan, backup keys/storage, monitored restore/rollback rehearsal | Production |

Country intent is EU-wide; unproven destinations must be explicit pending states in the market matrix and cannot accept payment. Do not silently reduce the agreed scope to NL/DE. Validate regional exclusions and bulky services, not just the country code.

Proposed engineering targets and legal obligations are different. Obtain qualified review of applicable VAT, consumer withdrawal/returns, product/importer information, textile labelling, packaging EPR, privacy/cookies and accessibility duties before launch. Do not treat a generic “custom order” checkbox as grounds to remove withdrawal rights. Sources in the commerce research are starting references and must be rechecked against the final product/countries.

Create `docs/product/market-readiness.csv` from real inputs during P0.2 with one row for each of the 27 countries and an explicit state (`pending`, `blocked`, `ready`). Country readiness is independent of UI language readiness. Do not fill unknown costs or promises with defaults that enable sales.
