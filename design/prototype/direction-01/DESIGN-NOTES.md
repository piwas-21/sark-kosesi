# Dicle — working design direction

Design proposal for Piwas, 23 October 2026. The brand name is undecided. This is a local interactive concept, not a deployed shop. Product images, prices, dimensions, stock and orders are illustrative. The supplied reference is not Piwas inventory.

## Design plan

- Colour: chalk #F5F3EE, madder #812D37, basalt #272B2B, pale stone #DEDCD3, olive #667256, white #FFFFFF. Dark mode uses basalt surfaces and pale stone text; photography stays unchanged.
- Type: Georgia for a sculptural, slightly softened wordmark and large short headlines; a system humanist sans-serif for useful product details. Arabic uses a platform Arabic font; do not letter-space Arabic. Production typography requires a licensed multilingual family and native-reader QA.
- Layout: a split opening with large, left-aligned words beside a full-height room photograph; narrow product controls beside generous imagery; a workshop-like room planner; a practical admin list. Mobile reorders content and uses a compact navigation row.
- Memorable element: red floor seating in a contemporary basalt-and-plaster interior. One woven geometric border establishes the textile reference; avoid ornamental wallpaper, generic bazaar imagery, and copied supplier branding.
- Motion: respond to configuration, drawers and theme changes. No scroll hijacking, autoplay carousel, or decorative background video. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Critique before building

A cream-and-serif boutique alone would look interchangeable. The revised direction anchors the page in a deep woven red, an explicit floor-seating silhouette, basalt architecture and a functional room-fit tool. Small decorative motifs were removed from cards and buttons. No invented artisan, handwoven, sustainable or customer-review claims appear as facts.

## Prototype scope

Storefront, product configuration, local basket, local favourite, dimension planner, illustrative admin tabs, system/light/dark preference and seven-language core storefront copy. Long admin text and some demonstration explanations remain English. All translations are drafts, especially Kurmanji. No checkout, emails, account creation, real inventory, room uploads or AR is connected.

The planner checks an illustrative rectangular footprint only. It does not assess doorways, furniture collision, wall shape, cushion compression, tolerances or real product dimensions.
